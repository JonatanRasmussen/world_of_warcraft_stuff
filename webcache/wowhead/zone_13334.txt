But as drought depletes the forests of Ardenweald, enemies seek to plunder the grove's potent magic. <h2 class="heading-size-3">Encounters</h2><ul>
<li><div><a href="/npc=164567/ingra-maloch">Ingra Maloch</a> – Cursed to exist outside the cycle of Life and Death, the Drust now seek to circumvent their fate using Ardenweald's mechanisms of Rebirth. Their would-be conquest has found its way to Tirna Scithe, where Ingra Maloch and his followers use foul magic to subjugate the local inhabitants.</div></li>
<li><div><a href="/npc=170217/mistcaller">Mistcaller</a> – Tirna Scithe is defended by force of arms, but also cunning. The playful Mistcaller has used the mists to confuse and disorient invaders. With the Drust attack the Mistcaller cannot easily tell friend from foe, and only after her puzzles and games are defeated can you safely reach the heart of Tirna Scithe.</div></li>
<li><div><a href="/npc=164517/tredova">Tred'ova</a> – The gorm have been chewing at the edges of Ardenweald, devouring desiccated husks of the dead. The beast called Tred'ova has gorged herself on something far more precious, the wildseed of Lakali, loa of knowledge. Now that Tred'ova has tasted the loa's power and wisdom, she hungers for more than survival.</div></li>
</ul>
